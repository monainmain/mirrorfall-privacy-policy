01

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Monamojis ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Android version of Mirror Fall distributed through Google Play. It covers information kept on your device, Google Analytics for Firebase Analytics, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Google Play Games Services.

02

Information stored on your device

Mirror Fall stores the following in the app's private local storage:

  • Your best score and the highest score offered to the leaderboard
  • Audio and reduced-effects preferences
  • Whether you completed the tutorial
  • Your language and notification-prompt preferences

We do not receive this local file. Clearing Mirror Fall's app data or uninstalling the game deletes it from your device.

03

Google Analytics for Firebase

Mirror Fall uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure how the game is used and to improve gameplay. Analytics automatically collects an app-instance identifier; app lifecycle, session, and screen-interaction information; app and device information such as app version, device model, operating system, language, and general device category; and approximate geographic information derived by Google from a masked IP address during collection. Mirror Fall does not request Android location permission or collect precise GPS location.

Gameplay events

We send these limited events and related values:

  • Game starts and how a run was started
  • Tutorial completion
  • Run endings, score, duration, waves seen, end reason, and whether the run set a new best
  • Opening the global leaderboard
  • Whether a leaderboard score submission succeeded, and the submitted score
  • Notification permission prompts, results, and settings changes

We do not send your name, email address, Google Play Games player ID, free-form content, or precise location in these Analytics events. Advertising ID and Android AdServices ID collection are disabled. Analytics events are marked unavailable for ad personalization. We do not set a developer-defined Analytics User-ID.

04

Firebase Cloud Messaging

Mirror Fall uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver occasional game reminders and updates. Firebase creates an installation identifier and registration token and processes app, device, operating-system, language, delivery, and notification-interaction information needed to route and measure messages. We do not store the registration token in the game's local save file or a developer-owned server.

On Android 13 and later, Mirror Fall asks for notification permission only after you choose Allow notifications in the game's explainer. You may choose Not now, deny the Android request, or change notifications at any time in Android's app settings. Declining notifications does not prevent offline play, local progress, or leaderboard use.

05

Google Play Games Services

On supported Android devices, Google Play Games Services may automatically authenticate the Play Games profile selected in your device settings. Google processes gamer identity (such as gamertag, avatar, and a game-specific player identifier), device and service information, SDK analytics and diagnostics, and game activity needed to provide and improve Play Games Services.

Mirror Fall uses Play Games Services only to submit completed-run scores and display the global leaderboard. If Play Games is not available or you are signed out in your device settings, you can still play Mirror Fall and keep a local best score. Mirror Fall does not create its own account, retrieve your email address, access your friends list, or store your Play Games profile or player ID in its local save file or Analytics events.

Leaderboard entries may be visible to other players according to your Play Games profile visibility and Google's service settings.

06

Why information is used

  • App functionality: deliver optional notifications and provide Play Games authentication, score submission, and the global leaderboard.
  • Analytics and product improvement: understand sessions, tutorial completion, gameplay balance, feature use, and aggregate engagement.
  • Diagnostics, security, and service reliability: maintain and troubleshoot Firebase and Play Games Services and prevent abuse.

Mirror Fall has no advertising or in-app purchases. We do not sell personal information or use the information described here to target advertising.

07

Service providers and disclosures

Google processes Analytics data to provide Google Analytics for Firebase. Our Analytics property is not linked to Google Ads, AdMob, AdSense, Ad Manager, Display & Video 360, Search Ads 360, or BigQuery, and Google Signals is disabled. Google may use limited de-identified or account-configuration information under the Analytics sharing settings we select and may use information as necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and support its services.

Google processes Firebase Cloud Messaging installation, registration, delivery, and interaction information to route and measure optional notifications.

Google separately processes Play Games profile, service, device, and gameplay information under Google's terms and privacy policy to provide and improve Play Games Services. We may also disclose information if required by law or necessary to protect users, our rights, or the security of the game and its services.

For details, review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Google Play Games profile privacy and settings

08

Retention and deletion

Analytics

Our Google Analytics property retains event-level data associated with identifiers for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. The 14-month period resets when new user activity occurs. These settings do not necessarily affect standard aggregated reports, and Google may retain service and security information as described in its policies. Uninstalling the app stops future collection from that installation but does not automatically erase information already processed by Analytics.

Cloud Messaging

Firebase retains installation IDs until we make an API request to delete them, after which removal from live and backup systems may take up to 180 days. Uninstalling Mirror Fall stops that installation from receiving future messages; Android settings can disable notifications without affecting gameplay.

Play Games

Google retains Play Games profile and game data under your Google account settings and Google's policies. You can view or delete Play Games data for Mirror Fall, or delete your Play Games profile, using Google's Play Games deletion controls.

Requests to Monamojis

You or a parent or guardian may email us to ask a privacy question or request access to or deletion of data we control. Mirror Fall does not link Analytics identifiers to a name or email address, so we may need information sufficient to locate a record and may not be able to match an email alone to a particular Analytics record. Where a request can be verified and matched, we will use available deletion tools and respond as required by applicable law.

09

Children's privacy

Mirror Fall is suitable for a general audience that may include children. It has no ads, purchases, chat, user-generated content, or direct collection of names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, audio, or precise location by Monamojis. Analytics uses the same limited, pseudonymous app and gameplay data for all players; we do not use it to build advertising profiles. Play Games availability and profile features depend on the Google account and device settings, including Google's controls for supervised accounts.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information that should be deleted, contact us. You may also use the Google Play Games deletion controls linked above.

10

Security and international processing

Firebase and Play Games Services encrypt the information described above in transit using HTTPS or TLS. Local progress is kept in Android app-private storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Google may process information on servers outside your country, subject to its terms, safeguards, and privacy policy.
