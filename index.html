01

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Monamojis handles information when you use the mobile game Mirror Fall. It applies to the Android version of the game distributed through Google Play.

02

Information we collect

Mirror Fall does not collect, access, transmit, sell, or share personal or sensitive user data. The game does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud storage, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Information stored locally

To remember your progress and settings, Mirror Fall stores the following information only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Best score
  • Audio preference
  • Reduced-effects preference
  • Whether the tutorial has been completed

Monamojis cannot access this information, and the game does not send it off your device.

03

Device features and permissions

Mirror Fall may use your device's vibration feature to provide optional gameplay haptics. This feature does not collect or reveal any information about you or your device.

04

Sharing and third parties

Because Mirror Fall does not collect or transmit user data, Monamojis does not share user data with other companies or individuals.

Google Play may independently process information when you download or use an app through its services. That processing is governed by Google's own privacy policy and is not controlled by Mirror Fall or Monamojis.

05

Security

Local game information is kept in app-private storage protected by Android. Mirror Fall does not transmit this information over a network. While no storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, limiting the information to your device reduces exposure.

06

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on your device until you clear Mirror Fall's app data or uninstall the game. Removing the app or clearing its data deletes this locally stored information. Monamojis cannot retrieve or restore it.

07

Children's privacy

Mirror Fall does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe the game is behaving differently from what is described here, please contact us.

08

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Mirror Fall's features or data practices change. The revised policy will appear on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes will be reflected in the game and its Google Play disclosures as appropriate.
